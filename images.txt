===================================================================
STYLE SPINE — append verbatim to the end of every prompt below
===================================================================
Documentary photography inside a working Nairobi software studio, late
afternoon falling into blue hour. Shot on a 35mm prime at f/2, camera
held at standing eye height, shallow depth of field that stays honest
rather than dreamy. One dominant light source: cool daylight from tall
west-facing windows on the upper right, falling off fast into deep
shadow on the left of frame. Grade cool and desaturated toward deep
indigo-blue, warm practical lamps held back to small specular accents
only. Fine 35mm grain, gentle highlight rolloff, lifted shadows, never
crushed black. Real room, real wear, unstyled, never glossy.

===================================================================
GLOBAL CONSTRAINTS — apply to every image
===================================================================
- Aspect ratio 16:9, render at 2560 x 1440.
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, signage, watermarks or UI of any
  kind rendered inside the image. All type is HTML on top.
- Compose so the lower-left third is dark, low-detail and uncluttered:
  the headline and body copy sit there. Push the subject and all
  visual interest to the centre-right and upper two-thirds.
- Keep the subject readable when centre-cropped to 4:5 portrait.
- No people looking directly into the lens.
- Consistent horizon and camera height across the set: horizon sits
  just above the midline, lens roughly 1.6m from the floor, level, no
  tilt, in every frame.

===================================================================
SEQUENCING NOTE FOR THE GENERATOR
===================================================================
Images 01 through 06 are six frames of one continuous dolly shot moving
forward through a single building: pavement, threshold, mid-floor,
meeting room doorway, workbench, shoulder. Camera height, lens, light
direction, time of day and grade do not change between them. If a frame
reads as a different room, a different day or a different photographer,
it is wrong — regenerate it against the STYLE SPINE rather than
adjusting the others to match.
